Think about the last time you snapped at someone you love over something small. The dishes. A tone of voice. A text that took too long.

The reaction was bigger than its cause. That's the tell. A movie just started.

Now notice this. Even in the middle of it, some part of you knew. Something in you registered, "this is too much." That something wasn't tight. It was watching.

I call that the balcony. Picture a theater. Down on the screen, the movie plays: the argument, the worry, the 11pm scroll. Up on the balcony there's a quieter seat. From there you can see the whole picture. You can see that it's a picture.

You don't have to climb up there and stay. You only have to remember the seat exists.

So here's the first noticing from the book, for today only. When a reaction is bigger than its cause, say to yourself, "a movie started." That's all. No fixing, no analyzing.
