How did the noticing go? If you caught even one movie starting, that counts. If you only noticed it afterward, that counts too. Almost everybody starts there.

Today I want to give you something small to do in the gap. I call it the Sacred Pause.

  1. Something lands. A comment, an email, a look across the room.
  2. Before you answer, take one breath. Just one, a little slower than you'd like.
  3. Notice where it landed in your body. Chest, jaw, stomach. You don't have to change it. Just find it.
  4. Then answer. Or don't.

That's it. It isn't meditation, and it isn't a trick to stay calm. It's a seat on the balcony for the length of one breath.

The pause itself is easy. Remembering to pause is the real work. So go easy on yourself when you forget. You will. I still do.
